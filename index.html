Steep

A week of loose thoughts, brewed into a few sentences.

Keep a few. Let the rest go.

Drop in pieces all week: a line from a meeting, a worry, a half-formed idea. At the brew time you pick, Steep turns the words into short sentences. You keep the few worth keeping. The rest are set aside, then deleted after a year.

How it works

  • Add a piece whenever something crosses your mind. Type it, dictate it, or share it from another app.
  • On your brew day, Steep writes short sentences from that week's pieces, along with a reason for each one.
  • Keep the sentences you want and tap Done. Your pieces and the sentences you didn't keep are set aside, then deleted after a year.
  • Steep can also bring back an old sentence, once a day by default. Keep it within a day, or it's gone.
  • At the end of the month, Steep groups the sentences you kept, you decide what each group becomes, and Steep writes up the month. Save it or send it somewhere, because it stays only until you finish the next month.

Your pieces live on your phone. There's no account. To write sentences, Steep sends the text of your pieces to Claude, Anthropic's AI. It asks your permission first. Read the Privacy Policy.
