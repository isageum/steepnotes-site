What the app does

You drop in pieces during the week. At the brew time you choose, Steep sends the text of those pieces to an AI and shows you the short sentences it writes. You keep the ones you want. At the end of the month, Steep groups the sentences you kept, and you decide what each group becomes.

Sentences are written by AI. They can be wrong, and they can miss what you meant. Check anything factual before you rely on it. Nothing Steep writes is medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

Steep lets things go on purpose

Letting go is how Steep works. When you finish a brew, that week's pieces and the sentences you didn't keep disappear from the app, and they're deleted a year later. An old sentence that comes back to you is gone if you don't keep it within a day. A monthly result stays only until you finish the next month, so save or send out anything you want to hold on to. Steep can't bring back what it has deleted, and neither can we, because we never had a copy.
